The country reports provide Daily Device Installs and Daily Device Upgrades as separate metrics. Each count covers its own reported dates. A 30-day count requires every date in the window. Upgrades and installs contribute to neither download badge. [Google Play report definitions](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/6135870?hl=en)

Google Play showed **1K+ downloads** on its public listing on 2026-11-26. This rounded milestone is excluded from both badges. [Google Play listing](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=io.github.friesi23.mhabit)

The [Apple App Store](https://apps.apple.com/app/table-habit/id6744886469) and [Microsoft Store](https://apps.microsoft.com/detail/9NG22PL73NGZ) listings are public. Their developer analytics have restrictions on disclosure, so this page excludes their private counts.
